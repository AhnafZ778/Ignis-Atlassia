MCD64A1 burned area

MCD64A1 Collection 6.1 monthly Burn Date and companion QA rasters. Retrieved and imported locally on 2026-09-30 from the University of Maryland fuoco SFTP archive (user guide §4). The calendar shows mapped pixels for matching months; this is lagged burned-area context, not active-fire truth, a pass mask, a cloud mask, or a fire perimeter. Independent review remains pending.

All 12 supplied Burn Date/QA pairs are inventoried: six support the California and Punjab–Haryana case months; six cover areas outside those demo footprints. Official Collection 6.1 QA rules now retain burned dates only on land with valid data; full-period unburned counts also require a full mapping period, no contextual relabeling, and no special condition. Same-date shared-grid counts are descriptive only: MCD64A1 uses active-fire data in its algorithm, and no match cannot establish fire absence. Independent review remains pending.

Target archive periods and what is still pending

For both areas, the row-only positive detections begin 2006-07 for MODIS and 2012-07 for Suomi NPP; request-metadata verified coverage begins in July 2022 and continues through the latest complete standard archive window. Row-only history does not establish continuous coverage, and missing dates remain unknown. The live ledger below distinguishes those targets from files actually imported. Partial 2026 months stay visible as partial and are not treated as full-year comparisons.

Use exact request dates and the requested area in each sidecar. A world-area request can feed both study boxes. Make adjacent one-year requests if FIRMS limits the date range; the importer marks a month complete only when the whole month and study box are covered. Product versions are read from source rows when possible; never guess a version.

No additional dataset is required for the FIRMS detection calendar. Native satellite fire masks and geolocation are needed only to claim cell-level pass, cloud, or clear-sky coverage. See the full local import instructions.
