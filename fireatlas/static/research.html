Exposure is unknown

A complete FIRMS export does not establish cloud-free satellite coverage. No rate is calculated until a mask is supplied.

Mask contract and rate definition

Use fireatlas-coverage-v1, the ease6933-centroid-1km-v1 grid, matching bbox, start_date, end_date, a Boolean synthetic flag, and explicit provenance and derivation method. Each cells row has source_id, date, integer grid_x/grid_y and status: observed, cloud, no_pass or unknown.

The rate is 100 × detected cell-days within the observed mask / observed cell-days in the supplied mask. Missing rows are unknown. Cloud, no-pass and unknown rows contribute no exposure. A detection in an explicitly unobserved cell is rejected as a conflict. A cell-day is observed only when the upstream mask processing establishes usable observation of that cell; a partial footprint or a FIRMS hotspot alone is insufficient. Upload validation checks consistency, not scientific provenance. No full-AOI or joint exposure is inferred.
