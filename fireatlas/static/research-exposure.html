A rate does not describe the full AOI or prove that undetected cells were clear.

Mask contract and interpretation

A cell-day is observed only when upstream processing establishes usable observation of that cell. A detection marked cloud, no-pass, or unknown is rejected as a conflict. Missing mask rows remain unknown. Synthetic masks are labelled separately and cannot be mixed with authentic detections.
