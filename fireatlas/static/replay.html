What this frame can support

An empty frame cannot establish a fire-free day. Clouds, smoke, pass timing, and missing exports can affect observations.

Calculation & interpretation

Eligible standard-processing vegetation-fire rows are assigned by detection centroid to the EPSG:6933 1 km grid and deduplicated within each source and UTC date. The 2D view uses a fixed radial smoothing kernel. The 3D view draws 1 km cell footprints colored by locally smoothed values. Colors are relative within each frame, so their brightness cannot quantify changes between dates.

Persistence counts distinct observed dates per cell. FRP is the highest selected-source report for the day, in megawatts; choose one sensor to use it. “Both” is the distinct union of source cells. Missing cells are not no-fire observations.

Landscape sources & acquisition periods
△
Terrain

Supplied NASADEM tiles provide the local relief overlay; streamed World Elevation supplies the 3D ground surface.

▧
Vegetation and land cover

Local MOD13Q1 NDVI is a 16-day 2024 composite. Local MCD12Q1 is annual 2024 IGBP land cover. Neither is an instantaneous fire-day measurement.

◉
Burn-date context

Local MCD64A1 July–August Burn Date is a later MODIS-derived product. It is contextual and not independent active-fire validation.
