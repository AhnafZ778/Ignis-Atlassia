Counting method & comparison limits

Detection centroids are assigned to the shared 1 km equal-area grid. A cell contributes once per UTC day across the selected products, even when several pixels or sensors detect it. Monthly totals sum these daily counts; raw pixels remain available separately.

Prior-year medians use at least three complete same-month exports with the same source cohort and area. This common-grid aggregation is a sampling proxy; it does not calibrate MODIS and VIIRS sensitivity. Complete exports do not establish cloud-free or continuous satellite coverage.

READ THE SIGNAL CORRECTLY

A hotspot is an observation.

A cell-day counts a common grid cell with one or more detected thermal pixels on a UTC day. It is a sampling proxy. It does not count individual fires or burned area.

Why two sensors? VIIRS can detect smaller hotspots than MODIS. Switch sources to compare their raw evidence; the joint view counts a shared cell/day once.

What does gray mean? Missing source exports or unknown satellite pass/cloud coverage must not be read as a fire-free day.
