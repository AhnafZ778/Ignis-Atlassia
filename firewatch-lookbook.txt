investigation workflow.

ART DIRECTION

Obsidian background / ember orange / warm white / satellite cyan. Use a large globe as the opening, then move into a precise 2D investigation workspace. Add a
responsive mobile layout and a reduced-motion setting. Every example map or metric should be identified as real data or a visual sketch.
