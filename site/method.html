Counting rules & historical comparison limits ↗
  1. Keep product, platform, version, native confidence and original CSV fields.
  2. Count FIRMS type 0 or missing across confidence levels. Other types remain inspectable but are excluded from calendar totals.
  3. Use the acquisition date in UTC. Assign the centroid to a 1 km EPSG:6933 cell.
  4. Count each cell once per UTC day across the selected sources. Sum daily counts for the month.
  5. Incomplete exports remain partial or unknown. Complete exports do not prove clear passes.
  6. Prior-year medians require three complete same-month exports with the same source cohort and area. Product-version differences make the comparison descriptive.
Hotspots are thermal anomalies. Cell-days are not burned area, perimeters, fire counts or calibrated fire risk.
