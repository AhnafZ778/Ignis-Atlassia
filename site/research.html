What this overview can and cannot answer

It can compare imported detections by source, date, and shared cell. It cannot tell whether every overpass saw the AOI, convert detections into a perimeter, infer a fire cause, or estimate sensor sensitivity without matched overpasses and independent reference data. Open Method for the calculation details.
